The #1 confusing thing

Finding your Grab at Terminal 1

E-hailing cars (Grab, AirAsia Ride, inDrive) can't stop at the arrival hall. They wait one floor down, outside the doors on Level 1. Book after you're out with your bags, so the driver isn't waiting.

  1. L3Exit Arrival HallAfter baggage + customs

    Collect bags and a free trolley. Use the toilets and baby room here first, it's quieter than downstairs.

  2. ↓Lift to Level 1Follow "E-hailing" / "Kenderaan e-panggilan"

    Lifts take trolleys and strollers. Escalators are fine if you only have backpacks.

  3. L1Door 3 or Door 4Designated e-hailing pickup

    There's a covered waiting area with seats. Now open the app and book.

  4. ✓Check before you get inPlate · car · driver name

    Match all three with the app. Never get in a car that just pulls up and offers a ride.

    Family & accessibility

    Moving with kids, prams and wheelchairs

    For parents

    • Baby care roomsIn the Main Terminal Building, the Contact Piers and the Satellite Building. Changing tables and a private space to feed.
    • Free trolleysFound at baggage claim. Small kids can't ride in them, so keep one adult on the trolley and one on the kids.
    • Use lifts, not escalatorsStrollers and trolleys are not allowed on escalators. Lifts are next to every escalator bank.
    • Agree a meeting pointPick one big sign (for example "Door 4, Level 1") and tell kids to go there and ask staff in uniform if they get lost.

    For wheelchair users & older travellers

    • Ask your airline firstRequest wheelchair help when booking. Staff can push you from the plane through to your ride.
    • Special assistance counterWhen departing, go to check-in counter Z16 for help. Airport wheelchairs are also kept at the gates.
    • Aerotrain to the SatelliteThe shuttle train between the Main Terminal and the Satellite gates is level boarding, no steps.
    • Step-free ride picksKLIA Ekspres and Grab (choose a larger car) are the easiest. Buses usually have steps.

    Stay safe

    Five rules that keep your trip trouble-free

    Say no to "Taxi? Taxi?"

    People who walk up to you inside the hall are touts (locals call them ulat teksi). Only use counters, the app, or the official rank.

    Match the plate

    Check plate number, car model and driver name in the app before opening the door.

    Pay at the counter

    For coupon taxis, pay inside at the counter. Never hand cash to someone outside.

    Share your trip

    Use "Share my ride" in Grab so family can follow the car live.

    Kids sit in the back

    Children ride in the back seat. Bring a travel booster if you can, cars rarely have one.
